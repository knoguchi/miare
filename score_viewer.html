Render and play back a miare arrangement

Pick a sample from the menu above — that is one click, nothing to choose on disk.

Or drop your own files here

  • a .musicxml score — the source, the arrangement, or both
  • its .miare.json report, to mark phrase boundaries and show diagnostics

Two scores open side by side: the first on the left, the second on the right. Drop a third and it replaces the right-hand one.

Then

Press Play (or Space). One transport drives both scores, and the playhead marks the measure each is in. Click a measure to jump there. Hear both / left / right switches which score sounds.

Serving it

Run python3 -m http.server in the repo root and open http://localhost:8000/score_viewer.html. The sample menu needs the server; drag-and-drop works either way.
